# XGC CORP Logo and Trademark Policy

The brand name **XGCERP** and the logo are trademarks of **XGC CORP**.

---

## Introduction

XGC CORP owns and oversees the trademarks for the XGCERP name and logos. We have developed this trademark usage policy with the following goals in mind:

- To make it easy for anyone to use the XGCERP name or logo for community-oriented efforts that help spread and improve XGCERP.  
- To make it clear how XGCERP-related businesses and projects can (and cannot) use the XGCERP name and logo.  
- To prevent anyone from using the XGCERP name and logo to unfairly profit from, trick, or confuse people who are looking for official XGCERP resources.  

---

## XGC CORP Trademark Usage Policy

Permission from **XGC CORP** is required to use the XGCERP name or logo as part of any project, product, service, domain, or company name.

We will grant permission to use the XGCERP name and logo for projects that meet the following criteria:

- The primary purpose of your project is to promote the spread and improvement of the XGCERP software.  
- Your project is **non-commercial** in nature (it can make money to cover its costs or contribute to non-profit entities, but it cannot be run as a for-profit project or business).  
- Your project neither promotes nor is associated with entities that fail to comply with the license under which XGCERP is distributed.  

If your project meets these criteria, you will be permitted to use the XGCERP name and logo to promote your project in any way you see fit, with one exception:  
**Do not use XGCERP as part of a domain name.**

---

## Allowed Uses

Use of the XGCERP name and logo is additionally allowed in the following situations:

- Businesses or projects may use the XGCERP name and logo **to refer to and explain their services**, but:  
  - They cannot use them as part of a product, project, service, domain, or company name.  
  - They cannot use them in any way that suggests an affiliation with or endorsement by XGCERP or XGC CORP.  

**Example:**  
✅ “123 Web Services, offering XGCERP consulting for small businesses.”  
❌ “The XGCERP Consulting Company.”  

- It’s acceptable to use the XGCERP logo as part of a page that describes your products or services.  
- It is not acceptable to use the XGCERP logo as part of your company or product branding itself.  
- Under no circumstances is it permitted to use XGCERP as part of a **top-level domain name**.  

We also do not allow the use of the trademark in advertising (including AdSense/AdWords).

---

## Our Intent

It is not the goal of this policy to limit commercial activity around XGCERP. We **encourage XGCERP-based businesses**, and we would love to see hundreds of them.

When in doubt about your use of the XGCERP name or logo, please contact **XGC CORP** for clarification.
